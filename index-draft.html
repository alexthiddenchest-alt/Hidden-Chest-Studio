Hidden Chest Studio is a Malaysian game development studio delivering interactive experiences across PC, mobile, and bespoke brand installations. Founded in 2020, we build in Unity and Godot, and ship on PC, mobile, and on-site hardware.

We've shipped interactive projects for AirAsia and Khazanah Nasional, alongside our own flagship IP, Midwest 90: Rapid City. Every project is managed directly by a co-founder. No middlemen, and we never subcontract your project out.
